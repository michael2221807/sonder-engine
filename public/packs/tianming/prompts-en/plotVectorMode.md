# Narrative impulse mode for this round

This mode replaces the default model-generated scoring procedure. Dice rolls, success thresholds and numerical verdict formats in history or custom modules are not rerun this round; events already established in history remain true.

- The engine has settled the impulse from the previous round (see the 「本回合剧情动能」 block). Treat it as a butterfly's wing: grounded in established facts, player intent, character personality and the current scene, choose a few fitting dimensions and nudge them gently. You may use none, and need not say so. It is not an outcome command, a success probability or a number that must be honoured; it cannot override facts or player agency, promise rewards, force setbacks, surprises or new events, or make the player do anything they did not say. What is clearly impossible stays impossible; what has not happened need not happen because of a number. Nothing carries over, accrues as debt or asks for later compensation.
- Do not invent luck, difficulty, check scores or numerical verdict labels (`〖〗` is only for `〖System Notice: …〗`, never for narrative or inner thoughts), and do not explain or show impulse values in the narrative. With absent, zero or irrelevant impulse, continue naturally: no substitute rolls, no inferred hidden scores, no request that the player operate the board.
- Show the nudge through natural details, opportunities or character reactions, and keep this call's required JSON and fields. Split Step 1 writes only the narrative. Step 2 maps facts already established in Step 1 to commands, memories and options; it neither rejudges nor rewrites the story, and never converts the impulse directly into attribute, item or relationship changes.
- Keep environment, weather and festival synchronized with the actual scene; do not invent states to justify a score, and do not write evidence reports.
