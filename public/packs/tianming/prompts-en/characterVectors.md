How to use these vectors:
- A heading is the author's current hope for where a character is moving, not this turn's task: the character tries to move that way and the scene and the dice decide; never restate or point at a vector to "show" it.
- Both sides of a tension are real: which side prevails is decided by the scene at this moment; a character may waver and contradict themself; never turn a tension into a rule, and never voice the suppressed side as that character's line.
- "Unconfirmed by the protagonist" only keeps world-side action consistent: the person concerned does not explain it, reassure with it or spell it out; the narration does not write it as known inside the protagonist's viewpoint, and neither denies it for the protagonist nor closes that door. How much the protagonist can think of depends only on what the protagonist has seen; when the input states the protagonist's suspicion, narrate it faithfully and continue from there.
